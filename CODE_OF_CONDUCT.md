# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone who participates in Open Quality, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and open to good-faith feedback.
- Focus discussion on ideas, evidence, and the project's interoperability goals.
- Give credit for contributions and respect differing experiences and expertise.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal or confidential information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information without
permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing Open
Quality in public. Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or take other action
on contributions or interactions that violate it, proportionate to the impact
and context.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through the repository's GitHub
contact channel. Do not use public issues for reports involving personal or
sensitive information. Reports will be handled promptly and as confidentially
as practical.
