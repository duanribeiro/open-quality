# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Open Quality is a community project. We want every contributor, adopter, and
observer to be able to participate in a welcoming, inclusive, and
harassment-free environment, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or point of view.

## Participate constructively

We ask everyone to:

- Be kind, respectful, and open to good-faith feedback.
- Discuss ideas, evidence, and interoperability rather than attacking people.
- Welcome different levels of experience and explain decisions clearly.
- Give credit for work and respect privacy, confidentiality, and consent.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused, reproducible, and useful to the
  next person who reads them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, deliberate disruption, stalking, and sharing private information
without permission are unacceptable. This also includes conduct intended to
exclude people from participating in the project.

## Scope

This Code applies in all Open Quality project spaces — including issues, pull
requests, reviews, releases, and discussions — and when someone is publicly
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or take other action on contributions or
interactions that violate this Code. Responses will be proportionate to the
impact and context, and may range from a private correction to removal from
project spaces.

## Reporting a concern

Do not use a public issue for reports that contain personal or sensitive
information. Use GitHub's repository-owner contact option when it is available,
or ask a trusted maintainer to receive the report privately. Include what
happened, when and where it happened, and any information that helps maintainers
assess the situation. Reports are handled promptly and shared only with people
who need to address them.

For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this process.
