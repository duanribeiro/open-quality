How to Model Software Quality with ISO SQuaRE

Connect ISO SQuaRE quality characteristics to software requirements, quality measures, and timestamped observations using Open Quality.

By Open Quality · November 24, 2026 · 2 min read

Teams need a shared vocabulary for quality before they can agree on what to measure. ISO/IEC SQuaRE provides models for organizing software product quality, quality in use, and measurement concepts. Open Quality uses those ideas to structure quality requirements in a version-controlled contract.

From a characteristic to a requirement

A characteristic names a broad quality concern, such as reliability or maintainability. A subcharacteristic narrows it: availability is one aspect of reliability, while testability is one aspect of maintainability.

The contract classifies a requirement under the relevant characteristic:

quality:
  characteristics:
    - characteristic: reliability
      subcharacteristics:
        - subcharacteristic: availability
          requirements: [api-availability]

The characteristic IDs come from the supported schema vocabulary. They are classification values, not project-defined resources. This gives teams a consistent way to browse and compare requirements across services.

Make the expectation measurable

A quality requirement contains the acceptance criterion. It references a quality measure and records the target for that requirement:

kind: QualityRequirement
metadata:
  id: api-availability
  name: API availability
spec:
  statement: The API must achieve at least 99.95% monthly availability.
  priority: critical
  qualityMeasures:
    - qualityMeasure: availability
      target:
        operator: greaterThanOrEqual
        value: 99.95
        unit: percent

The measure describes the quantity and may define a calculation. Its inputs can be QualityMeasureElement resources containing values and timestamps. Keeping the target on the requirement allows two requirements to use the same measure with different acceptance thresholds.

Separate the model from collection

The contract can describe a measure and its inputs, but it does not prescribe how a monitoring system, test runner, or team collects the data. Likewise, the reference CLI does not execute a measurement expression or gather telemetry; it evaluates values provided in a runtime snapshot.

That boundary keeps the quality model portable. A team can keep its measurement system while making requirements, targets, and workflow checks reviewable in Git.

For the formal models, see ISO/IEC 25010 for product quality and ISO/IEC 25019 for quality in use. Open Quality provides a contract format that draws on these concepts; a valid contract is not, by itself, a claim of ISO conformity.
